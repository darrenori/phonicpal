## Scope and mode

Three surfaces share one world. `index.html` is the B2B landing page (Persuade).
`app/index.html` is the learner app (Operate, child-facing). `educators/index.html` is the
educator dashboard with sample data (Operate).

- Audience and job: SpED coordinators and school leaders decide whether to pilot. Children
  aged 5 to 12 practise alone. Educators check that practice is happening and helping.
- Action: on the landing page, try the learner app or open the educator demo. In the app,
  practise a word through See, Hear, Tap, and Say. On the dashboard, see who needs which
  sounds.
- Proof: a live, interactive word bar built from any typed word. There are no invented
  customers, statistics, or prices.
- Unresolved: contact channel, pricing, and pilot partners. Hide what is not decided.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A word is a bar model. Upside builds every word and every sum from manipulatives
the child can see, hear, tap, and say. This refuses the pastel card grid and mascot hero,
and it refuses clinical calm too.

OWN-WORLD: A pale cool mat (#EDF1EE) with a faint unit grid. It is a fixed kit where each
colour means one thing, with hard edges and no gradients:
- cobalt rods: tall letters, tens, and action rods
- lemon unit cubes: small letters and single quantities
- vermilion drop blocks: hanging letters and parts taken away
- leaf: the whole, answers, and Sparky
- ink navy: structure

Blocks rest on one shelf line and hanging letters drop below it. Empty places are drawn as
dashed outlines. The piece you hold lifts with a soft contact shadow. The type is Lexend,
with Lexend Exa for display.

STORY: The visitor sees a real word get built and split into syllables and sounds. They
come to believe this is structured literacy a child can do alone. Then they try the app.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The headline sits top-left. A full-width word bar spells "butterfly" in
blocks that drop onto the shelf, split into but, ter, and fly under a whole brace. A
type-any-word field sits under the bar. The "Try the learner app" rod button sits beside
the headline, and Sparky stands at the end of the bar.

FORM: Manipulatives Mat, number 5 on the ordered list, seed a2da91e8. The signature
interaction is split and join: tap the whole to split it into syllables, tap a part to lift
it and hear it. The motion grammar is settle-and-lift.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
